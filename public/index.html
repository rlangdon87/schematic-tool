What can I paste?
Free text, one quote per line, the same way Quick Add reads it:
  • $25M xs $25M on its own line starts a layer
  • P$25M or Primary $25M is the primary layer
  • Chubb 25% @ $1.2M means 25% at a 100% price of $1.2M
  • Zurich 20% prem $240k is the premium for that share
  • Allianz 15% p/o $50M xs $50M puts the line on that layer
  • ind or indication marks a non-firm line; declined is listed but not placed
  • Text in (brackets) becomes a note
Or paste a table from Excel with headers such as Layer, Limit, Attachment, Market, Share and Premium.
